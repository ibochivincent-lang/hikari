❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how Hikari redefines liquid yield on Stellar

What is hXLM?

hXLM is an ERC-4626-compatible yield-bearing token on Stellar Soroban. As yield compounds from lending and AMM fees, 1 hXLM becomes worth more XLM over time.

How does withdrawal work?

Withdrawals use a Lido-style queue. When you request a withdrawal, you receive a claim ticket. Ready tickets can be claimed instantly from the liquid buffer.

How are AI agents restricted?

Agents cannot withdraw funds to arbitrary addresses. They can only execute pre-approved Soroban rebalancing transactions that pass all formal on-chain safety invariants.
