Hikari Protocol FAQ

What is hXLM?

hXLM is the SEP-41 compliant yield-bearing liquid staking token on Stellar. Holding hXLM continuously earns compounded yield from Blend lending, Phoenix CLAMM fees, and Jito MEV backruns.

How does unbonding work?

Under Turbo Mode, unstaking requests are settled immediately from the 15%+ liquid reserve buffer. If the queue is congested or market volatility spikes, requests are queued in FIFO order to protect the vault.

What is GateSeal and Dual Governance?

Adopted directly from Lido, GateSeal is a one-shot circuit breaker that pauses contract allocations for 7 days during critical anomalies. Dual Governance empowers stakers with veto rights against malicious DAO proposals.
